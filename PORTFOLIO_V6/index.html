About

Freerunner turned editor

Starting out as a freerunner, I fell in love with recording and editing my own videos.

I used my training clips as a playground to learn video editing: experimenting, failing, and improving with every project. What began as a passion quickly turned into a skill, and eventually into a profession.

Those early self-made videos became the foundation that led me to pursue a career in visual storytelling.
